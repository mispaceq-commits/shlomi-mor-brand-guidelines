Origin

Shlomi Mor has worked in the hair and beauty industry for more than twenty years. The house was founded in New York City and operates from a single atelier — every wig is made there, by hand.

Material

Each piece is built from the highest quality virgin European hair and customized in texture, color, and fit — for a seamless, natural look and lasting comfort.

Service

Lace-top wigs, medical wigs, full lace wigs, toppers, halo and ponytail extensions. Consultations are available in person in New York City and remotely through the website.
