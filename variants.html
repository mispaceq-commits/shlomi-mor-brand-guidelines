Brand Foundation 04
Foundation

Mission. Vision. Values.

01 Mission

To return confidence.

Every woman who comes to Shlomi Mor leaves with the feeling of having her dream hair back — whether for a season, a stage, or a lifetime.

02 Vision

Made by hand. In New York.

A house where medical solutions and quiet fashion sit on the same shelf, with equal care. We do not have a hurry.

03 Values

Quiet, exact, kind.

Presence over performance. Precision over decoration. Empathy over selling — the brand reads as it is.

Typography · In Use 14
Type System

Three voices, one page.

Three typefaces, each with a job: display, functional, signature. Never mixed by accident.

A Display Serif
Mor
Didot — Headlines & Mastheads

Reserved for covers, section openers and the brand mark. Never use below 28 px. Tracking: −1 % · weight 400 / 700.

B Functional Sans
Aa
Futura LT — Body, UI, Labels

Everything that has to work. Set body at 15 px / 1.7, labels in +0.32 em tracked caps at 11 px.

C Script Accent
crown
Snell Roundhand — Signature Only

One word per layout, maximum. The signature note — tag lines, dedications, hand-written accents on packaging. Never set as a sentence, never below 36 px.

Pairing rule

Big serif on top  ·  small caps sans underneath  ·  one script word as a quiet accent.
