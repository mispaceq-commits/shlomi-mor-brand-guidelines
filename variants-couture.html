How we speak

Quiet, exact, kind.

We have nothing to prove. We do not shout, we do not sell. We make the wig and let the woman speak for it.

  • Made by hand, in New York.
  • Each piece is customized in texture, color, and fit.

  • Virgin human hair, nothing else.

We do not say
