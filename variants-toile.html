Foundation

MISSION·VISION·VALUES

A short manifesto. Read once, kept always.
01 — Mission

To return confidence.

Every woman who comes to Shlomi Mor leaves with the feeling of having her dream hair back — for a season, a stage, or a lifetime.

02 — Vision

Made by hand. In New York.

A house where medical solutions and quiet fashion sit on the same shelf, with equal care. We do not have a hurry.

03 — Values

Quiet, exact, kind.

Presence over performance. Precision over decoration. Empathy over selling — the brand reads as it is.

Type System 14
Type System

THREE VOICES

Display, functional, signature — each with a job.
A — Display Serif
Mor
Didot — Headlines & Mastheads

Reserved for covers, section openers, brand marks. Never under 28 px.

B — Functional Sans
Aa
Futura LT — Body, UI, Labels

Everything that has to work. Body 15 px / 1.7. Labels tracked +0.32em.

C — Script Accent
crown
Snell Roundhand — Signature

One word per layout, maximum. Hand-written accents only. Never under 36 px.

Pairing Rule

Big serif on top  ·  SMALL CAPS SANS underneath  ·  one script word as a quiet accent.
