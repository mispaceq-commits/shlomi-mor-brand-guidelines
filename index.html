Introduction 03
Chapter I

Hello, there.

a brand
of confidence

Shlomi Mor Wigs is a New York atelier dedicated to the craft of fine custom human-hair wigs. For more than two decades, we have served women who wish to refresh their natural look, and women who must rebuild it — through medical hair loss, treatment, or recovery.

Two intentions live inside one house: restoration and refinement. In both, our promise is the same — a wig that feels like you, only quieter and more beautiful.

This brand book is a love letter to that promise. It defines the visual and verbal language that holds every piece of our communication together: editorial, generous in white space, precise in detail, and always — first — human.

— Written for the team and the partners of Shlomi Mor Wigs, NYC.

Brand Streams 05
05 — Pillars

Two Houses, One Atelier.

Shlomi Mor Wigs speaks in two registers. The same craft, the same hands, the same NYC studio — but two emotional tones, two visual languages, two ways of meeting our client.

Stream 01

Medical Hair Loss

For women navigating chemotherapy, alopecia, trichotillomania or post-natal hair loss. The voice is warm, supportive, human. The visual language uses soft light, pale tones, skin-close textures and intimate framing. Every word and image protects dignity.

Lead with   Care · Reassurance · Privacy

Stream 02

Luxury Custom Wigs

For everyday women and stage-ready celebrities (Katy Perry, Emma Roberts, Kim Kardashian). The voice is editorial, confident, glossy. The visuals borrow from Vogue and Harper's Bazaar: deep contrast, exact typography, statement portraits.

Lead with   Status · Craft · Fashion

Logo Construction 07
07 — Construction

Built on a Grid.

Clear Space

x = cap-height of "S"

Maintain a clear space of at least x on every side of the logo. No type, image or graphic element may enter this zone.

Minimum Size

32 px digital · 18 mm print

Below this size, switch to the monogram variant. The descriptor line is dropped below 24 mm.

Alignment

Optical, never mechanical

The script word wigs sits optically centered below the wordmark, not mathematically. Always trust the eye.

Color Usage 10
10 — Application

A quiet ratio.

Color should never crowd a layout. Use the palette in the proportion below, with white and signature black holding the majority of the canvas.

Proportional Rhythm

60 / 25 / 10 / 5

White and ink lead. Champagne and gold accent. Rose and blush enter for warmth, never as a base.

60%  White
20%  Ink
10%  Champagne
PaperInkChampagneBlushGold
Do
Generous white space
  • Set ink type on cream or paper for editorial calm.
  • Use champagne for accents, gold for borders or stamps.
  • Pair blush with cream — never with rose at full strength.
  • Use stone gray for secondary type and meta information.
Avoid
Crowded color fields
  • No rainbow combinations — max three brand colors per surface.
  • Never set type smaller than 12 px in rose or blush.
  • Do not invent tints; use only the six brand colors.
  • Avoid pure cyan or magenta — the brand stays on its palette.

Rules
  • One display headline per surface — never two competing serifs.
  • Eyebrows always sit in tracked sans-serif, never serif italic.
  • Sacramento accents go above the serif headline, slightly tilted inward.

Soft, supportive, human

Window light, off-white walls, women at home or in private studio. Eye contact, half smiles, hands. Never clinical. Never staged "before/after". The wig is part of the woman, not the subject.

Strobe-lit studio black, single-color seamless, sculptural posing. Detail macros of texture, parting, hairline. Strong negative space. Borrow the cadence of a fashion editorial.

Tone of Voice 17
17 — Voice

How we speak.

We speak the way a kind friend who happens to be the best in the industry would speak — calm, exact, never selling, always seeing the woman first.

We are
  • Warm — not sentimental
  • Precise — not technical
  • Confident — not loud
  • Discreet — not cold
We are not
  • Salesy or transactional
  • Clinical or pitying
  • Trendy or slang-heavy
  • Promising the impossible
Yes — we say

“A wig that feels like you, only quieter and more beautiful.”

No — we don’t say

“Get the hair of your dreams — click now, limited offer!”
